The Little Book of

1

Dora & Cura

Meet the twins! Dora wears her red pigtails tied up like little bat wings, and Cura never goes anywhere without his red bow tie. These two baby vampires have big ruby eyes, tiny fangs and even bigger hearts. They share almost everything (except the swing, maybe).

2

The Family

Baby brother Bio is the newest (and loudest) vampire in the house: his pacifier never stays in for long. Mom Bella and Dad Draco keep everyone fed, safe and tucked in, and they always have enough hugs to go around, even when Dora and Cura get a little jealous.

3

Home Sweet Home

A two-storey brick house with a garden, a fountain and a garage. Inside: Dora's pink room, Cura's starry blue room, a cozy living room with a fireplace, a kitchen with Bio's high chair, a bat-themed bathroom, a baby room and a giant playroom.

4

Friends Next Door

The neighbourhood is full of monster families: the big-hearted Frankens, the Werewolves (who usually look just like people) and the Mummies, who don't have mouths, except when it's time to brush their teeth!

5

Playtime

A slide, a ball pit, a little haunted toy house and a pumpkin cushion: the playroom is where every song starts. Bio builds wobbly block towers with the twins (and knocks them down again), then everyone lines up the plushies, from cuddly bats to friendly spiders.

6

Watch & Sing

Sing along with Dora and Cura on YouTube! Short songs and longer compilations about eating well, sharing, safety and family. Pick a video, press play, and don't eat the slime.
